# FretLab 🎸

A guitar and bass practice app for students at Allegro Charter School of Music — tab and notation playback, loop and speed trainer, tuner, Game mode, and scored practice recordings.

## Use it
- **Online (always the newest version):** https://csauer-allegro.github.io/FretLab/
- **Offline copy:** [Download FretLab.html](https://csauer-allegro.github.io/FretLab/download.html), then open it in Chrome. Download again when your teacher says there's a new version.

Works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on Chromebooks and laptops. The microphone and camera are only used for the tuner, Game mode feedback, and recordings — nothing is uploaded. Recordings stay on your device until you turn them in.

## Opens
Guitar Pro (.gp, .gp3–.gp7, .gpx), MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml, .mxl), and alphaTex (.txt).
